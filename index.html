The legacy

More than pedigree — a philosophy of greatness.

The Mejiro lineage is remembered not merely for blood and breeding, but for the temperament, power, and poise that define true racehorse excellence. It is a lineage shaped by resilience — horses that carry themselves with confidence, with focus, and with a rare competitive spirit.

Across generations, the Mejiro name has become a benchmark for style and substance. It represents the elegance of movement and the steel of ambition — a legacy that continues to inspire admirers of the sport.
